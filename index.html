IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION+

Do not use if you are allergic to eptidoneran or to any of the ingredients in RYMVAIL®.

Before using RYMVAIL, tell your healthcare provider about all your medical conditions, including if you have eye problems, have a parasitic (helminth) infection, are scheduled to receive any vaccinations (you should not receive a “live vaccine” right before and during treatment), are pregnant or plan to become pregnant, or are breastfeeding or plan to breastfeed. Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take. Do not change or stop your other medicines, including corticosteroid or asthma medicines, without talking to your healthcare provider.

RYMVAIL can cause serious side effects, including allergic reactions (which can sometimes be severe), eye problems, inflammation of your blood vessels (rarely, in people with asthma), psoriasis, and joint aches and pain. Stop using RYMVAIL and get emergency help right away for signs of an allergic reaction such as breathing problems, swelling of the face, lips, mouth, tongue or throat, fainting, hives, or a rash that looks like a bullseye.

The most common side effects include injection site reactions; eye and eyelid inflammation, redness, swelling, itching, eye infection, dry eye or blurred vision; cold sores in your mouth or on your lips; and a high count of a certain white blood cell (eosinophilia). Side effects vary by condition. RYMVAIL is not used to relieve sudden breathing problems and will not replace an inhaled rescue medicine, or to treat any other forms of hives. Report side effects to the FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088.
